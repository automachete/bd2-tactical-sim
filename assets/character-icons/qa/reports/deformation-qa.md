# Character icon deformation QA

Deformation is evaluated independently from feature fidelity and transparency. Existing generated icons are evaluated but are not regenerated solely for style-strength differences, per user direction. Newly generated icons must meet all of the following:

- The head and face are visibly enlarged relative to the shoulders and torso.
- Head-attached canonical traits such as horns, ears, cap emblems and hair ornaments are judged by their size relative to the enlarged chibi head. Proportional enlargement with the head is expected and must not be misclassified as an invented feature enlargement.
- Shoulder width and visible torso height are compressed into an icon-like bust silhouette.
- Eyes and defining face shapes remain readable at 64 px and 32 px without changing eye state or inventing marks.
- Canonical clothing coverage, exposed-skin areas, anatomy-defining silhouette, accessories and left/right asymmetry are preserved; deformation must not become censorship or redesign.
- A realistic or near-original body proportion is not accepted as sufficiently deformed for newly generated work.
