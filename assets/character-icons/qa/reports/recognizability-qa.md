# Character icon recognizability QA

Scope: the contact sheets are used only to test visual recognizability after downscaling. They are not used to judge whether a character feature is correct. Feature additions, omissions, counts, left/right placement, clothing coverage and eye state are evaluated separately from each individual original-resolution reference in `feature-inventory.md` and `feature-qa.md`.

Method:

- Each final transparent PNG was downscaled independently with high-quality Lanczos sampling to 64 × 64 px and 32 × 32 px.
- The transparent thumbnails were composited on one neutral dark background with identical spacing. Printed names are navigation labels and were ignored when judging the icon artwork.
- A 64 px PASS requires the major face/head and costume cues to remain visible.
- A 32 px PASS requires at least one reliable silhouette cue plus a reliable palette/contrast cue to remain distinguishable from the rest of the set.
- Final result: 61/61 PASS at 64 px and 61/61 PASS at 32 px.
